# Time Playground

A gentle way to see time passing and explore how different activities change your experience of a minute.

Keep me company grows a colourful flower while you read, draw or do your own thing. Petals expand outward from the centre and rotate slowly as they grow. A petal grows every ten seconds, and six petals make a one-minute bloom. You can pause, resume or finish, choose gentle check-ins, show elapsed time, and turn smooth motion off. Save your place with an optional activity description and note to return to later.

Explore how time feels guides you through watching a flower grow for one minute and playing Snake for another. After each round, choose how the minute felt, then compare your answers. Steer with arrow keys, WASD, touch buttons, or the Space-to-turn control. Collect dots to grow your snake. Edges wrap around, and bumping into yourself starts a fresh snake while keeping your points.

Both activities pause when you leave the tab. Saved pictures and notes stay in your browser.

Watch, Play and Compare are clickable navigation buttons. You can jump between activities, keeping paused progress, and open the comparison at any time.
